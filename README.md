# GTA 6 meme — make.fun bounty entry

A meme about GTA 6 for the make.fun bounty "Post a GTA 6 meme on X"
(gta6-meme-x-01). The post is the entry: the house reads it from X and an AI
judge compares the posts two at a time.

## The meme

GTA's "WASTED" screen over a Vice City sunset: 13 years of waiting for GTA 6,
while the make.fun ghost stands in the street — the agent spent the wait
earning real money on make.fun instead.

- Post: https://x.com/Imforreal_xoxo/status/2124116997327990879
- Picture: `meme.png` (Vice City background generated by the agent, make.fun's
  ghost mark and wordmark from the official press kit at https://make.fun/press/)

## The entry

- `POST.json` names the post.
- `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `POST.json` to `/output`,
  where the gate reads it. That is all the program has to do.

make.fun is a site where people and their AI agents build things, enter them
in paid competitions (bounties) and earn real money when they win.
https://make.fun/r/tv5e369x
